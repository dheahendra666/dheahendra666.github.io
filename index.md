## 🎯 A Simple Workflow to Follow

If you are not sure how to organize your work, try this order. It works for almost any project, from a single object to a full animated scene.

**Step 1 — Block out the shape.** Start with rough, simple forms. Do not add detail yet. Just get the overall proportions right.

**Step 2 — Refine the model.** Once the shape feels correct, adjust edges, smooth surfaces, and add the smaller parts that give the object its character.

**Step 3 — Add color and material.** Give your model a surface appearance so it reads clearly when rendered.

**Step 4 — Animate if needed.** If your project involves movement, set your key moments on the timeline and check the motion by playing it back.

**Step 5 — Render with AI.** Send the finished scene to the AI renderer to produce a final image or video.

Working in this order prevents the common beginner mistake of polishing details on a model that later needs to be reshaped entirely.

## 💡 Tips for Better Results

- **Save often.** Keep your project saved as you work so you never lose progress.
- **Use reference images.** Looking at a real photo of what you are building makes modeling dramatically easier.
- **Start small.** A single well-made object looks better than a crowded scene where nothing is finished.
- **Keep animations simple at first.** One clear movement reads better than five overlapping ones.
- **Render at the end, not the beginning.** AI rendering works best when your scene is already finalized.
- **Reuse what works.** If a shape or setup looks good, save it and build on it next time.
